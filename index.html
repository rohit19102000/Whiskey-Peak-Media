Frequently Asked

Answering common questions.

What is your typical build timeline? +

Our custom projects typically take between 4 to 8 weeks. This timeline includes our deep strategy and discovery wireframing phase, high-fidelity UI design cycles, creative GSAP animation coding, optimization, and final deployment.

Do you build websites on WordPress or Webflow? +

We believe in high performance and complete creative freedom. We primarily build bespoke codebases (HTML, Javascript, React, Next.js) hosted on fast CDN servers, or custom Webflow builds for clients who require self-managed systems.

Will our website perform well in Google search (SEO)? +

Yes, absolutely. We automatically implement semantic HTML tags, optimize image layers (WebP, fetch priority), compress bundle payloads, and avoid render-blocking assets. Every project aims for perfect 100 scores in Lighthouse audits.

Do you offer website maintenance and updates? +

Yes. We offer dedicated hosting, server monitoring, security patches, and monthly content/design updates to ensure your digital platform remains in pristine condition and performs cleanly day after day.
